by_pts = [points, first-choice applicants, permits issued in bonus + 1-2 pass at that level (includes 2nd-choice draws)]. min_pts = lowest point level that drew in the bonus (max-point) pass.
T.tags = authorized permits (Draw Report); NR.tags = nonresident cap (Draw Report NonRes 'Authorized Permits'). Residents may fill all permits, so R.tags is omitted. Without a Draw Report (2025 fall), T.tags = permits issued from Draw Odds.
Species: deer hunt types containing 'whitetail' -> C; other deer (incl. 'Any antlered deer') -> D. Youth-only hunts skipped. AZ reuses hunt numbers for different hunts, so hunts are matched across years by species+units+method+sex and nearest season-open date (<=14 days); id = most recent number. 2026 hunt metadata comes from the 2026 regulation booklets (2026 draw odds not yet published).

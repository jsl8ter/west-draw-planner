Your strategy

Goal: two hunts a year, plus a better-quality tag every couple of years. That means two buckets. The hunt-now bucket uses general tags, OTC tags and high-odds draws. The point bank buys points or lottery chances every year in the states where quality tags take time.

Next 60 days

  1. Wyoming preference points by Mon, Nov 2. Elk $52, deer $41, antelope $31 at wgfd.wyo.gov. They jump to $75 each on Jan 1, and Wyoming only gives points you buy.
  2. Arizona OTC archery deer tag (2027). Usually goes on sale in early November at otcarchery.azgfd.com and sells out. $160 license + $300 tag. Good for Coues or mule deer in Aug or Dec–Jan.
  3. Arizona hunter ed point. Take the online "Ethically Hunting Arizona" course before the February draw. It's a permanent extra bonus point for every species.
  4. Idaho general tag draw. Buy the $185 license on Dec 1, apply Dec 5–15 ($18). Pick an uncapped elk zone (McCall, Weiser River, Panhandle).
  5. Montana preference point. $100 by Dec 31 for the general combo draw. Makes a 2028 combo very likely.
  6. Make accounts on all 9 state portals now so the spring deadlines are just a few clicks.

Year by year

2027

HuntIdaho general elk, uncapped zone (McCall or Weiser River). Random draw in December; leftover tags often sell OTC in late March as a backup.

HuntNew Mexico Coues deer, archery in Unit 23 or 24 (about 75% nonresident odds in 2025). Backup: the Arizona OTC archery tag.

OptionWyoming pronghorn in a 1-point area (24 Thunder Basin, 15 Clearmont or 2 Sundance) using the point you buy this week. Much of that country is private, so line up access before applying.

BankBuy Wyoming elk and deer points each summer. Apply or buy points in AZ (elk, pronghorn, deer), UT (LE elk, deer, pronghorn), NV (elk, deer, antelope) and CO (elk, deer, pronghorn; points are free in 2027). Each one is a small lottery ticket at a top unit.

2028

HuntMontana general combo (elk + deer) with 2 preference points, about 85–95% odds. Hunt any general district, such as the Gravelly Range.

HuntColorado pronghorn or deer, mid units (San Luis Valley 79/82 pronghorn, or 22/31 deer). Colorado moves to a 50/50 preference/random split in 2028, so 1–2 points plus the random half are enough.

2029

Trophy-ishArizona Coues rifle, Units 31/32 or 36. By then you'll have 2 earned points + the hunter ed point.

HuntWyoming general elk, Region E (Bighorns), about 3 points in the regular draw. Or Idaho general elk/deer, or Utah OTC archery elk.

2030

Trophy-ishColorado elk, Units 66/67 (Lake City) or Unit 2 archery, using 3+ points.

HuntNevada antelope (mid units like 041/042 or 071–079) with 3–4 squared bonus points, or a Montana combo again.

2031+

TrophyUtah LE elk on Manti or Wasatch (archery, about 5–10 points), and Arizona elk Unit 6A archery. Keep the long-shot applications going every year: AZ 1/9/23 elk, UT Beaver East, NV 231, MT Missouri Breaks.

Yearly cost to stay in the game

Licenses plus application or point fees, before any tag you actually draw. Tags are charged only if you draw, except New Mexico and Montana, which charge up front and refund.
